Module 04 · Payroll Processing

Prepared, checked, and approved
— never skipped

Each payroll period and branch moves through five sequential sign-offs. Skipping ahead isn't possible, and stepping back always leaves a reason.

PrepareThe initial computed sheet is assembled for a branch and period.
PREPARE PAYROLL
CheckA second reviewer verifies every computed figure.
CHECK PAYROLL
NoteNoted by the next level, with comments attached if needed.
NOTE PAYROLL
VerifyA final check against attendance and contribution records.
VERIFY PAYROLL
ApproveThe period is locked in — final reports can now be generated.
APPROVE PAYROLL
Revert, with a reason. Any step can be sent back, but only with a required comment explaining why — logged alongside the approval trail.
